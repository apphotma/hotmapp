Your Safety and Privacy

At HOTMAPP, your safety and the privacy of your data are our highest priorities. All chat communications and files sent in secure chat rooms are end-to-end encrypted (E2EE), meaning only you and the recipient can read them.

Key Features:
  • End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): Uses industry-standard cryptographic keys generated on-device.
  • Presence Visibility: Choose who can see your online status and last-seen timestamp.
  • Device Verification: Verification of secondary devices via QR code scans ensures only authorized devices access your account.
  • Secure Backups: Your local message cache is fully locked.

For security concerns, please contact our security response team immediately via Contact Support.
